Andamio

21 "scaffold" structures: conjugate one small verb, add any infinitive, and you can say a lot.

Each structure unlocks when you finish the lesson that teaches it. Practice turns English into Spanish: say it aloud or type it, then check.

Necesidad y obligación

3.1

necesitar + infinitivo

to need to · taught in lesson 3.1

  • Necesito comprar fruta.I need to buy fruit.
  • ¿Necesitas ir al mercado?Do you need to go to the market? (tú)
4.2

tener que + infinitivo

to have to · taught in lesson 4.2

  • Tengo que trabajar mañana.I have to work tomorrow.
  • ¿Tienes que estudiar hoy?Do you have to study today? (tú)
6.2

hay que + infinitivo

one must / you have to · taught in lesson 6.2

  • Hay que tomar el metro.You have to take the metro. (in general)
  • Hay que dar vuelta a la izquierda en la esquina.You have to turn left at the corner. (in general)
9.3

deber + infinitivo

should / ought to · taught in lesson 9.3

  • Debe tomar mucha agua.You should drink a lot of water. (usted)
  • Debes descansar.You should rest. (tú)
