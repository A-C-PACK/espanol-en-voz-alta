Repaso B1

B1 review

In this unit

15.1

Despedida

I can use everything from B1 together: tell stories, give opinions, deal with problems and talk about my plans and work.

120 min

Grammar

  • Review: the present subjunctive after quiero que, no creo que, ojalá, espero que, cuando, me preocupa que, te recomiendo que
  • Review: preterite, imperfect, pluperfect and present perfect in one story
  • Review: tú and usted imperatives, affirmative and negative; accidental se; the conditional; que, donde, quien, lo que
  • All 21 Andamio structures

Words

  • A farewell: la despedida, el brindis, ¡Salud!, extrañar
  • When something goes wrong at a restaurant: se me cayó, la charola, cortesía de la casa
  • A video call: tocar, deslizar, colgar, cargar el celular

Culture

  • From Mexican Spanish to Chicago Spanish: Spanish in the world and in the United States
