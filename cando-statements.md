# CEFR Can-Do Speaking Statements (A1–B1)

Task-level can-do statements derived from the CEFR Companion Volume (Council of Europe, 2020).
Official descriptors are generic; these are concretized into practicable tasks, each tagged with its source scale.
★ = especially suited to AI voice role play. Target variety: Mexican Spanish.
