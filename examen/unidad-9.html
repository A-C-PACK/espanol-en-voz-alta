Prueba de la unidad 9

Unit 9 test: the weekend, problems with things you bought, and health

This test checks the three goals of unit 9: talking about last weekend (9.1), explaining a problem and asking for a fix (9.2), and describing symptoms at the doctor's or the pharmacy (9.3). It also uses numbers and prices, object pronouns from unit 7 and tener que.

In listening tasks, try without the transcript first: it's there for accessibility. Auto-scored parts give you a percentage (70% = superado). Writing and speaking come with a model answer and a checklist. Anything you miss goes into Repaso.

El sábado fui a Puebla a la fiesta de quince años de Fernanda, la hermana de Diego. ¡Estuvo buenísima! Cenamos mole, vi el vals de Fernanda y bailé hasta la una. El domingo regresé a la ciudad muy cansado.

Y el lunes empecé a sentirme mal en el trabajo: me dolió mucho la garganta y en la noche tuve fiebre. El martes fui al consultorio de la farmacia de la esquina. La consulta costó sesenta pesos. El doctor Méndez me dio paracetamol y un consejo: tengo que descansar. Por eso ayer no fui a trabajar. Doña Lupita me hizo un caldo de pollo buenísimo, y hoy ya me siento mucho mejor.

¿Y tú? ¿Qué hiciste el fin de semana?

Un abrazo,

Alex

Reply to Mike

Reply to Alex's email as if you were Mike, or write to a friend about your own week: what you did last weekend (where, with whom, how it was), and one small problem you had (something broken, or you felt sick) and what you did about it.

Expresión oral Speaking

Checks: lesson 9.2, lesson 9.3

At the store and at the doctor's

Play both scenes aloud (record yourself if you can). Use usted in both.

  1. Store: the headphones you bought on Saturday don't work. You have the receipt. Explain the problem and ask for an exchange or a refund.
  2. Doctor: you've had a headache and a fever since yesterday. Explain your symptoms, say when it started, and ask what you have to do.

Prepare for a minute, then record yourself (or speak aloud). Then listen to the model and check your answer.
