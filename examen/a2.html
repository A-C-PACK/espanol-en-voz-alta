Examen A2

A2 level exam, in the style of the DELE A2

This exam checks the whole A2 level in the four skills, like the official DELE A2 exam of the Instituto Cervantes (its tasks are written for this course). Suggested time: about 90 minutes. Do it in one sitting, without the lessons. 70% on the auto-scored parts means you have reached A2.

In listening tasks, try without the transcript first: it's there for accessibility. Auto-scored parts give you a percentage (70% = superado). Writing and speaking come with a model answer and a checklist. Anything you miss goes into Repaso.

Tarea 2 · A day that didn't go as planned

Write a short story about a trip or a day out when something went wrong: where you went and with whom, what the place and the weather were like, what happened, how it ended, and what you'll do differently next time.

70–90 words.

When you have finished, check your text:

Show a model answer

El mes pasado fui a Tepoztlán con unos amigos. Era domingo y había muchísima gente en el pueblo. Primero comimos quesadillas en el mercado y después empezamos a subir al Tepozteco. Hacía mucho calor y no teníamos agua: ¡se me olvidó en el coche! Entonces una señora muy amable nos dio dos botellas. Al final, llegamos arriba muy cansados, pero la vista era increíble. La próxima vez llevaré más agua y saldré más temprano.

Expresión e interacción orales Speaking · 15 min

Tarea 1 · Describe a photo · Tarea 2 · Role play

Tarea 1 (2–3 minutes): look at the photo and talk about it. Describe the place and the people: what they look like, what they're wearing and what they're doing. Then imagine who they are, what they're talking about, what they did before and what they're going to do later. Finish by comparing this place with a place you know. Tarea 2: you're staying at a hotel. The air conditioning in your room doesn't work. Explain the problem to the receptionist (since when, what you did), ask politely for a solution, then ask how to get downtown.

  1. Photo: the place and the people (En la foto hay…, tiene el pelo…, usa lentes, lleva = is wearing) and what they're doing (están platicando).
  2. Photo: imagine who they are and what they're talking about (Creo que…), what they did before (preterite) and what they're going to do later (van a…).
  3. You: compare the place with a place you know (más … que, tan … como) and say what you usually do there (suelo…).
  4. Role play: greet, explain the problem (no sirve, desde…, ayer funcionó y hoy ya no), ask for a solution (¿me podría…?), ask for directions, thank.

Prepare for a minute, then record yourself (or speak aloud). Then listen to the model and check your answer.
