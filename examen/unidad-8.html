Unit 8 test: plans, invitations and apologies, a hotel

This test checks the three goals of unit 8: making plans with a friend (8.1), invitations and apologies (8.2), and booking a hotel room or checking in (8.3).

In listening tasks, try without the transcript first: it's there for accessibility. Auto-scored parts give you a percentage (70% = superado). Writing and speaking come with a model answer and a checklist. Anything you miss goes into Repaso.

Reply to Mariana

Expresión oral Speaking

Checks: lesson 8.1, lesson 8.2, lesson 8.3

A voice message and a phone call

Record two short messages (about one minute in total). 1) A voice message to a friend: apologize because you can't meet today, give a reason, say what you're doing right now, and suggest another day, time and place. 2) A phone call to a hotel: book a room (dates, nights, kind of room) and ask two questions.

  1. Apology + es que… + estar + gerundio.
  2. A new plan: ¿Por qué no…? / ¿Te late…?, ¿quedamos a las… en…?
  3. Hotel: quisiera…, del … al …, number of nights, sencilla / doble.
  4. Two questions: price, breakfast, check-in, or ¿me podría…?

Prepare for a minute, then record yourself (or speak aloud). Then listen to the model and check your answer.
