Prueba de la unidad 4

Unit 4 test: time and dates, free time, weather and postcards

This test checks the three goals of unit 4: time, days and dates (4.1), free time and how often (4.2), and the weather and short messages (4.3).

In listening tasks, try without the transcript first: it's there for accessibility. Auto-scored parts give you a percentage (70% = superado). Writing and speaking come with a model answer and a checklist. Anything you miss goes into Repaso.

Gramática y vocabulario Language

Checks: lesson 4.1, lesson 4.2, lesson 4.3

1

Type the missing wordsComplete the sentences.

  1. 1:30 → la una y media.
    It's half past one.
  2. Nosotros en la fonda los viernes. (comer)
    We eat at the fonda on Fridays.
  3. ¿Qué tú los domingos? (hacer)
    What do you do on Sundays?
  4. Hoy mucho calor.
    It's very hot today.
  5. ¿ venir a las ocho? (poder, tú)
    Can you come at eight?
  6. El sábado que trabajar. (tener, yo)
    On Saturday I have to work.
  7. Mi cumpleaños es el de julio. (1st, in words)
    My birthday is on the first of July.

Expresión escrita Writing

Checks: lesson 4.3

A postcard from your weekend

Write a postcard to Mariana from a weekend trip: where you are, the weather, what you do there every day, your plan for tomorrow, and when you come back (day, date and time).

40–70 words.

When you have finished, check your text:

Show a model answer

Querida Mariana:

Te escribo desde Valle de Bravo. Aquí hace sol en la mañana, pero en la noche hace frío. Todos los días camino por el lago y como en el mercado. Mañana voy a andar en bici. Regreso el domingo dieciocho a las ocho de la noche.

Un abrazo,

Alex

Expresión oral Speaking

Checks: lesson 4.1, lesson 4.2, lesson 4.3

Your week

Talk for about one minute: your weekly routine and free time (with how often), what the weather is like where you live, and your plans for next weekend (day, time).

  1. What you do on weekdays and on the weekend; how often.
  2. The weather now and in winter or summer where you live.
  3. One plan with a day and a time; one thing you have to do.

Prepare for a minute, then record yourself (or speak aloud). Then listen to the model and check your answer.
