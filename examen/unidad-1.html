Prueba de la unidad 1

Unit 1 test: greetings, introducing yourself, personal details

This test checks the three goals of unit 1: greeting people and choosing tú or usted (1.1), introducing yourself (1.2), and giving personal details (1.3). Take it without looking back at the lessons.

In listening tasks, try without the transcript first: it's there for accessibility. Auto-scored parts give you a percentage (70% = superado). Writing and speaking come with a model answer and a checklist. Anything you miss goes into Repaso.

A message to the language-exchange group

Write a message to the Coyoacán language-exchange group to introduce yourself, like Kenji. Include: a greeting, your name, age, where you're from, where you live, your job, the languages you speak, and a goodbye.

Expresión oral Speaking

Checks: lesson 1.1, lesson 1.2, lesson 1.3

Introduce yourself

You meet Rocío, the director of a language school, for the first time. Greet her with usted and talk for about one minute.

  1. Greet her (it's the morning) and say nice to meet you.
  2. Your name, and how to spell your last name.
  3. Where you're from and where you live.
  4. Your job and the languages you speak.
  5. Your age and your phone number.
  6. Say goodbye.

Prepare for a minute, then record yourself (or speak aloud). Then listen to the model and check your answer.
