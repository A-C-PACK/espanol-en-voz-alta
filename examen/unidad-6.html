Prueba de la unidad 6

Unit 6 test: ordering at a taquería, directions, bus tickets

This test checks the three goals of unit 6: ordering food and asking about the menu (6.1), asking for and giving directions with a map (6.2), and buying a bus ticket (6.3). It also uses numbers, prices and times from units 3 and 4.

In listening tasks, try without the transcript first: it's there for accessibility. Auto-scored parts give you a percentage (70% = superado). Writing and speaking come with a model answer and a checklist. Anything you miss goes into Repaso.

Expresión escrita Writing

Checks: lesson 6.1, lesson 6.2

Tacos near your home

A friend is coming to your neighborhood on Friday evening. Write a message: recommend a taquería (what to order, what's in it, the salsas), and give directions from the nearest metro or bus stop with tú commands and at least one ordinal.

60–100 words.

When you have finished, check your text:

Show a model answer

¡Hola, Mariana! El viernes vamos a la taquería Los Cuates. Te recomiendo el campechano: lleva suadero y longaniza. A ti te encanta el chile, entonces la salsa roja es para ti: ¡pica mucho! Para llegar, toma el metro hasta Etiopía. Sigue derecho por la avenida dos cuadras y, en la segunda calle, da vuelta a la izquierda. La taquería está en la esquina, enfrente de un parque. ¡Te espero a las ocho!

Expresión oral Speaking

Checks: lesson 6.1, lesson 6.2, lesson 6.3

Taquería, directions and a ticket

Play the three scenes aloud (record yourself if you can). Use usted with the taquero, the police officer and the ticket clerk.

  1. Taquería: ask for a recommendation, ask what's in a dish and if a salsa is spicy, order with con todo / sin… and para llevar.
  2. Street: you're at the Zócalo. Ask a police officer how to get to Bellas Artes and repeat the directions to check.
  3. Terminal: buy a round-trip ticket to Puebla for Friday; ask the time and the platform, and ask a question with ¿Se puede…?

Prepare for a minute, then record yourself (or speak aloud). Then listen to the model and check your answer.
