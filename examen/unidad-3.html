Prueba de la unidad 3

Unit 3 test: shopping, ordering food, likes and dislikes

This test checks the three goals of unit 3: buying at a market (3.1), ordering a meal (3.2), and saying what you like (3.3). It also uses numbers, tener and hay from units 1 and 2.

In listening tasks, try without the transcript first: it's there for accessibility. Auto-scored parts give you a percentage (70% = superado). Writing and speaking come with a model answer and a checklist. Anything you miss goes into Repaso.

Expresión escrita Writing

Checks: lesson 3.2, lesson 3.3

Reply to Mariana

Reply to Mariana's chat about Saturday: say yes, say what you'd like to eat and drink there, say one thing you love and one thing you don't like, and ask one question about the restaurant.

30–60 words.

When you have finished, check your text:

Show a model answer

¡Sí, perfecto! Quiero la cochinita y una sopa de lima. Para tomar, un agua de horchata. Me encantan los postres, pero no me gusta mucho la comida muy picante. ¿Aceptan tarjeta? ¡Y para Diego, cochinita sin cebolla! 😄

Expresión oral Speaking

Checks: lesson 3.1, lesson 3.2

At the market and at the fonda

Play both scenes aloud (record yourself if you can). At the market you have 150 pesos; at the fonda, order the set lunch.

  1. Market: greet, ask the price of two things, ask for quantities, pay and check the change.
  2. Fonda: order a soup, a main dish and a drink; ask if something is spicy; ask for the check.

Prepare for a minute, then record yourself (or speak aloud). Then listen to the model and check your answer.
