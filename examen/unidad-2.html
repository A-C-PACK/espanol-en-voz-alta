Prueba de la unidad 2

Unit 2 test: family, home, finding places

This test checks the three goals of unit 2: talking about family (2.1), describing where you live (2.2), and asking where things are (2.3). It also uses the greetings and numbers from unit 1.

In listening tasks, try without the transcript first: it's there for accessibility. Auto-scored parts give you a percentage (70% = superado). Writing and speaking come with a model answer and a checklist. Anything you miss goes into Repaso.

Expresión oral Speaking

Checks: lesson 2.1, lesson 2.2, lesson 2.3

Your people, your place

Talk for about one minute (record yourself or speak aloud). Then, imagine a stranger asks you where the pharmacy is on the map, and answer.

  1. Your family: how many brothers and sisters, names, ages, where they live.
  2. Your home: house or apartment, rooms, your room.
  3. Your neighborhood: what there is nearby.
  4. Answer: Disculpe, ¿dónde está la farmacia? (use the map).

Prepare for a minute, then record yourself (or speak aloud). Then listen to the model and check your answer.
