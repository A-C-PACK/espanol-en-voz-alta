Prueba de la unidad 7

Unit 7 test: daily routines, describing people, shopping for clothes

This test checks the three goals of unit 7: describing your daily routine and your job (7.1), describing family, friends and colleagues (7.2), and shopping for clothes (7.3).

In listening tasks, try without the transcript first: it's there for accessibility. Auto-scored parts give you a percentage (70% = superado). Writing and speaking come with a model answer and a checklist. Anything you miss goes into Repaso.

Expresión escrita Writing

Checks: lesson 7.1, lesson 7.2

An email to a new language-exchange partner

A new language-exchange partner wants to know about your life. Write an email: your job or studies, your typical weekday (in order), and a description of one important person in your life (looks, character, how he or she is these days).

70–100 words.

Expresión oral Speaking

Checks: lesson 7.1, lesson 7.2, lesson 7.3

My days, my people, my clothes

Talk for about one minute: your typical weekday; then describe a friend or relative as if you were showing a photo. Finish with a short shop scene: ask for a piece of clothing in your size, try it on and say how it fits.

  1. Your weekday in order: get up, work or study, come back, go to bed; one thing you usually do.
  2. One person: looks (es…, tiene…, usa…), character, and how he or she is these days.
  3. In a shop: color and size, ¿Me lo / la puedo probar?, me queda…, the price.

Prepare for a minute, then record yourself (or speak aloud). Then listen to the model and check your answer.
