Start lesson 1.1 How the course works

  1. Listen first

    Every lesson starts with real conversation, voiced by Mexican speakers, then shows you how it works.

  2. Then use it

    Practice step by step, then speak and write for real: a role play and a short text in every lesson.

  3. Keep it

    Repaso brings words and phrases back just before you forget them, across the whole course.
