Créditos

Course

Written and built for this course, adapting material from Español en voz alta (the speaking course), Andamio (scaffold structures), and the CDMX game. Syllabus based on the Council of Europe's CEFR Companion Volume (2020) and the Instituto Cervantes Plan curricular (2006).

Voices

All voices are synthetic, generated on a local machine with Qwen3-TTS from designed or previously approved voices; no real person's voice was cloned. The cast and how it was chosen are documented in the project's VOICES.md.

Illustrations

Illustrations were made with Ideogram 4 (non-commercial license) and checked by hand.

Fonts

Fraunces (Undercase Type) and Source Sans 3 (Adobe), both under the SIL Open Font License.
